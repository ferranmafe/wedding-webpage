# Wedding Webpage Design Document

## Tech Stack
- **Framework**: React
- **Build Tool**: Vite
- **Architecture**: Single Page Application (SPA)

## Theme & Aesthetics
- **Theme**: Argentinian Asado and Catalan Vermut.
- **Visual Style**: 
  - **Color Palette**: A warm, appetizing off-white or light cream background (#FDFBF7 or similar) contrasted with a burnt orange/terracotta-red (#D3543A or similar) for all lines, illustrations, and text.
  - **Typography**: Hand-drawn, playful, and slightly imperfect fonts (e.g., using Google Fonts like 'Gochi Hand', 'Amatic SC', or a custom handwritten webfont) that give an artisanal, laid-back vibe.
  - **Illustrations & Patterns**: Line-art drawings of classic treats (empanadas, choripan, churros, flancito mixto, mate, sifón for vermut). These will be placed strategically around the sections, along with small sparkles and vintage swirls.
  - **Overall Feel**: "Riquísimo Todo Che" – casual, fun, inviting, and highly thematic.

## Structure
The SPA will be divided into three main sections:

### 1. Home Section
- **Purpose**: A welcoming landing view.
- **Content**: 
  - Announcement that we are getting married!
  - Names of the couple.
  - A prominent date and location teaser.

### 2. Details Section
- **Purpose**: Inform guests about the logistical details of the wedding.
- **Content**:
  - **Agenda/Activities**: The plan for the day (e.g., welcome vermut, asado lunch/dinner, party).
  - **Date & Time**: Exact timings.
  - **Location**: Venue name, address, and maps.

### 3. RSVP Form Section
- **Purpose**: Confirm attendance and gather necessary guest information.
- **Content**:
  - Name(s) of attendees.
  - Attendance confirmation (Yes/No).
  - Open questions (e.g., dietary restrictions, transportation needs, song requests).
